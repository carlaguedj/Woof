    About us

    Our story.

    WOOF is a French brand born from a simple idea: your dog is part of your world, so why shouldn’t they be part of your style too?

    i.

    The French touch

    Inspired by French fashion, Parisian life and the little details that make everyday moments special, WOOF creates accessories designed to celebrate the bond between dogs and their humans.

    Our pieces are made to feel like more than just pet accessories. They are little expressions of personality, designed to fit naturally into your own wardrobe and lifestyle. From elegant collars to playful details, every design brings together fashion, comfort and that unmistakable French touch.

    ii.

    Made to match

    Some of our favourite pieces are made to match. Our dog collars and scrunchies are designed in the same fabrics, colours and prints, so you and your dog can share a look without ever feeling too “matchy-matchy”. Just a subtle little detail that says you belong together.

    Discover the pieces

    iii.

    Little moments

    Because at WOOF, we believe the best accessories are the ones that tell a story — the morning walks, the café afternoons, the weekends away, the spontaneous adventures and all those little moments that make your dog so much more than a pet.

    For them. For you. For us.

    WOOF is about celebrating that bond, one beautiful detail at a time.
