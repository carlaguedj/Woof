About WOOF

Our story.

WOOF was born from a simple idea: that the moments with our dogs are the most beautiful ones.

A morning coffee on a terrace, a long walk through the park, a stranger who stops to say hello. We wanted to make objects worthy of those moments — for them, and for you.

Good dogs, good people.

i.

Born on a café terrace

Inspired by European life — Parisian streets, Sunday markets, long walks and slow mornings — WOOF imagines accessories with the eye of a fashion house and the heart of a dog lover.

Nothing that looks like a pet shop. Everything that looks like you.

ii.

Made to be matched

A collar and a bracelet cut from the same ribbon. A bandana and a scrunchie in the same print. Our pieces are designed in pairs, because matching always looks better.

The Matching Edit

iii.

Details make the story

Soft velvet, gold-tone charms, a handwritten card and a little gift in every order. We work in small batches, choosing materials that are comfortable for dogs and made to last.

Comfort first

Soft linings, light hardware, sizes made for real necks.

Timeless design

Colours and shapes you will still love in ten years.

Small batches

Fewer pieces, chosen with care, finished by hand.
