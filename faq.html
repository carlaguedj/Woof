    i. About WOOF

    WOOF is a French-inspired lifestyle brand creating accessories designed to celebrate the bond between dogs and their humans. Our pieces combine fashion, everyday practicality and playful details, with a particular focus on accessories that allow you and your dog to share a look.

    WOOF was created with the idea that your dog is part of your world — so their accessories can be part of your style too. Rather than traditional pet-store accessories, WOOF pieces are designed with a fashion and lifestyle perspective, inspired by French style, Parisian life and the details of everyday moments.

    WOOF is a French-inspired brand built around French fashion, Parisian lifestyle and the idea of effortless everyday elegance.

    ii. The Matching Collection

    The Matching Collection is designed around the idea of sharing a little detail with your dog. Selected dog collars and human accessories are made in matching fabrics, colours and prints so you can coordinate without looking overly “matching”.

    Not at all. The idea is subtle coordination rather than identical outfits. A matching scrunchie, for example, can be a small detail that connects your look to your dog’s collar.

    Yes. Each piece is shown with its own price, and each collection also has a set price for the collar and its matching accessory together. The prices are shown on every product page.
